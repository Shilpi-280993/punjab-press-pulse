What is going well

  1. Government events led front pages in its own words.The 100 mini buses made the most front pages of any story; the Valmiki Tirath grant ran in 12 papers.
  2. Police action is the largest positive theme.Seizures and arrests, many in district editions, with DGP and SSP quotes.
  3. The water and federal-rights line is landing.River-water resolutions on TOI's front page; "BJP wants to snatch Punjab's water" in all three languages.

What needs improvement

  1. The opposition dominates the conversation.Opposition attacks are the top negative theme; AAP's own counter-attacks are about a quarter of their volume.
  2. Drugs is becoming the opposition's main weapon.No government answer to the drug-survey demand; the chemists' halt is now a patient-access story.
  3. Moga, Jhota and the OSD need responses.Crime and drugs each made front pages; Jhota is in its third week with Akal Takht prayers on 28 October.
